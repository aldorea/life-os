## The Core Problem — Organización

This is the frente that conditions all others. Without it, the other five compete for unmanaged time and attention.

The problem has two faces:

1. **Self-boundary failure.** When Sito says "I close at 18:00," he sometimes doesn't. The override is internal — not external pressure. No system can enforce this; the system can only surface it.
2. **Execution without direction.** Starting to work without clarity on what needs to happen. Activity without intention.

What "organized" means in practice: propose → prioritize → assign a day → protect a focus block → respect the boundary when it arrives. Not productivity theatre: sovereignty over time.
