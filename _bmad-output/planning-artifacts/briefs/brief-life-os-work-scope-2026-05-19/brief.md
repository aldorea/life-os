## Role Identity

Sito operates across two simultaneous professional contexts:

**Consultoría (Orbitant)**
Full-stack developer on a large-scale digitalization engagement for a financial and fiscal management company. Currently rotating across backend, frontend, and architecture depending on need — a deliberate pattern, not a fixed track. Tenure in the role: established and trusted (peer feedback confirms autonomy and respected technical judgment). Reports to: architect.

Transition in progress: moving into the **referente role for Portal del Cliente**, a new project within the same engagement. This role has not formally started but is the clear next step. The team and client-facing process for this portal are both being defined in parallel.

**Health Startup (Previene)**
Co-founder carrying the full product and technology surface. Two partners cover content. Backed by Basque Government funding. App in initial phase.

## Responsibilities Currently Owned

**Consultoría**
- Full-stack development: backend, frontend, and architecture-level work as needed
- Discovery and refinement ownership for assigned features, end to end
- Client communication in a supporting capacity: meetings, follow-up, clarification
- Architecture decision documentation (ubiquitous language, ADRs)
- AI tooling adoption and enablement within the team — recognized referente

**Startup**
- App design and development (sole technical contributor)
- Product definition: MVP scope, UX, quality/cost balance
- Legal compliance for a health-domain app

---

## Responsibilities in Transition — Active Step-In

**Consultoría — Portal del Cliente referente**
The path is open: the next step is to own Portal del Cliente end to end. Concretely:
- Lead discovery and refinement directly with the client interlocutor (being defined)
- Schedule and run client meetings independently
- Define and maintain the team's working process with the client
- Own deployment planning and production follow-through
- Unblock the team; be the single point of clarity for the portal

The gap is not competence — it is not having formally assumed the role. Everything needed to step into it already exists.

**Startup**
- Define and own the MVP roadmap with milestones
- Establish and verify legal compliance framework
- Identify and track government funding milestones (unknown — see Gaps)

## What Is Explicitly Not Mine

**Consultoría**
- Client's internal prioritization decisions — Sito owns the discovery process and ensures the right questions get asked; the client owns the answers and the business decisions
- Executing others' work — Sito enables and unblocks; he does not substitute for team members
- Managing client-side unforeseen events and delays — he can surface them and adapt, but cannot absorb organizational chaos that originates on the client side

**Startup**
- Content strategy — owned by the two content partners
- Fundraising and investor relations beyond the current government grant

## Known Gaps and Ambiguities

1. **Portal del Cliente team undefined** `[blocking]` — Who is on it, how many people, what profiles — all TBD. Cannot fully own team dynamics until this is resolved.
2. **Client-facing working process in flux** `[watch]` — Both sides are defining ticketing, refinement cadence, roadmap horizon, and planning rhythm simultaneously. Opportunities to shape the process exist — but only before the defaults are set.
3. **Funding milestones unknown** `[blocking for cadence design]` — The Basque Government funding has conditions and a timeline; neither is currently known. Accountabilities cannot be designed around a funding horizon that is not visible.
4. **Role assumption pending** `[fuzzy — not a true blocker]` — The referente transition for Portal del Cliente has not formally started. The undefined state is not a blocker — the role can be stepped into before it is fully described. Waiting for definition is a choice, not a constraint.
